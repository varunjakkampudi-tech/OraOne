# OraOne product portfolio

The product numbers are identifiers, not a fixed launch or development sequence. Product 1 is the active foundational product. Products 2–9 are planned. Product 10 is the long-term vision.

Viswatra is documented only as the intended portfolio-level brand direction; this repository does not establish a legal corporate relationship or ownership structure.

## Product 1 evidence boundary

The current repository contains code for website agents, chat and public API routes, knowledge ingestion and retrieval, leads, workflows, integrations, billing foundations, tenant administration, RBAC, widget delivery, and webhook foundations. A similarly named screen, route, or prototype does not make a future standalone product launched or commercially available.

Production availability is not claimed here. Live identity, provider, payment, external webhook, backup/restore, deployment, and cost-control gates remain separate evidence requirements.

## Shared ecosystem foundations

The intended future ecosystem may share identity, tenant and project governance, an AI gateway, billing and usage concepts, design-system components, reusable integrations, unified administration, and cross-product interoperability. These are architectural direction, not proof that every product or capability exists today.

Do not publish fictional release dates, completion percentages, customer numbers, or availability claims.
